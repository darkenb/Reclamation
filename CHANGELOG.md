# Changelog

## 1.0.2

- The converter's point recipes stay out of the replicator, also with Multifunction's "all handcraft" on. The replicator planned Dark Fog items through them: a large count needed more points than a number can hold and the queue stopped for good. The items they make also no longer show "points -> item" as their own recipe in their tips.
- Points and heat have their own icons (a token with a gem, a flame) instead of the accumulator's.

## 1.0.1

- New package icon.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Every item is worth points by its raw materials.
- Trash Converter: burns any item for points and makes the Dark Fog item its recipe asks for.
- Incinerator: burns any item for power. Fuels burn for their heat value; anything else gives energy and power by its rarity.
- Both are fed by sorters, by hand or with the fill key, and list what they have burned.
- Technology "Reclamation" unlocks both.
